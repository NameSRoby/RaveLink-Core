Local TTS

Install and manage local speech components for Alerts / TTS. Packages download only when selected and come from the official RaveLink GitHub repository.

No speech engine or voice is included in the RaveLink installer. Speech remains unavailable until a compatible engine and at least one voice pack are published and installed. Lighting and visual alerts continue to work without them.

Available TTS packages

Engine and voice packages appear here after they pass license, integrity, resource, and Windows playback review.

Where speech can be used

TTS is being designed as an alert action for Bits, Channel Points, subscriptions, gifts, raids, follows, Hype Trains, approved donations, Song Request events, manual tests, and future trusted triggers. Each alert will select its own voice, text source, volume, rate, cooldown, and queue behavior.

Custom voices

Custom Voice Tools will be a separate advanced download. It will require permission from the recorded speaker, process locally by default, and provide delete controls. It will not include celebrity or public-figure voice presets.
