Calibration & output settings

The highest output your lights may use. Loud audio and impacts can reach this limit.

Play a representative passage at your usual comfortable volume. Calibration captures the selected source in Preview, never changes playback volume and never controls lights. It sets a fixed reference; quiet passages stay quiet.

Calibration options · 30 seconds with 0 dB headroom

Include both quieter and louder passages. A short quiet sample can make later music sit at the ceiling and lose detail. If calibrated loudness stays at 100%, use a longer sample or more headroom. The reference stays fixed after calibration.

Keeps the calibrated response when the selected app's Windows mixer gain changes. System volume is recorded for context; app capture is independent of speaker volume. Multiple routes or sessions with different gains need a new calibration. In-player volume controls may require recalibration.

Steady evaluation checks the audio up to the selected limit and holds the lights when their target barely changes. Brightness follows sustained audio; color reactivity increases with intensity and slows through sustained quiet. The maximum is 12 per second. Adaptive cadence is experimental and varies the evaluation rate with the audio. Hue over REST shares a bridge budget; Entertainment streaming allows faster Hue updates.
